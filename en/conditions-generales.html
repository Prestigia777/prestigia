2. Services offered

Prestigia provides private chauffeur services (transfers, city-to-city and international transfers, chauffeur by the hour, events, private tours), car rental with or without a chauffeur, and bespoke services.

3. Services carried out by partners

Some services are carried out by selected partners: vehicle services (cleaning, protection, wrapping, servicing, MOT inspection), accommodation, admission tickets, professional guides and other bespoke services. For these, Prestigia acts as an intermediary: it coordinates the request and, where applicable, moves the vehicle. The service itself is performed under the partner's responsibility.

The partner's name is provided to the client on request. Where the partner applies its own terms, these supplement these terms and are provided to the client before booking.

4. Quote requests

Requests can be made through the website form, by email, by phone or via WhatsApp. The client provides complete and accurate information: the price in the quote is based on this information.

The quote is valid for 48 hours. After this period, the price and availability must be confirmed again by Prestigia.

5. Booking and confirmation

The booking becomes firm once the client has accepted the quote in writing and the deposit has been received. Prestigia then confirms the booking in writing.

A request sent through the website does not constitute a booking. Prestigia may decline a request, in particular if unavailable, if information is incomplete or for any legitimate reason.

6. Prices

Prices are those stated in the quote, in euros. The agreed price does not change with traffic.

Unless otherwise stated in the quote, the following may be charged as extras: waiting time beyond the time included in the price, additional stops, detours and kilometres requested by the client, tolls, parking fees and any service not included in the quote.

Prices given to business clients may be stated excluding VAT. Prices given to consumers are stated including VAT.

7. Deposit and payment

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, in particular for corporate accounts and travel agencies, a deposit of 50% of the quoted amount is required at booking. The balance is paid as set out in the quote.

Companies receive a detailed monthly invoice and travel agencies one invoice per booking file, according to the terms agreed with them.

Payment is made using the methods stated in the quote or on the invoice.

8. Late payment

Invoices are payable by the stated due date. For business clients, any late payment automatically and without formal notice incurs late-payment interest at the rate set by the Belgian Act of 2 August 2002 on combating late payment in commercial transactions, as well as a fixed compensation for recovery costs.

For consumers, the rules of the Belgian Code of Economic Law on amicable debt recovery apply. Prestigia may suspend any new service until outstanding amounts have been paid.

9. Waiting time

The following waiting time is included in the price:

  • airport: 60 minutes from the actual landing of the flight;
  • train station: 20 minutes from the actual arrival of the train;
  • address: 15 minutes from the agreed pick-up time.

Beyond this, waiting time is charged at €60 per hour excluding VAT, per started 15-minute block, i.e. €15 per block.

10. Changes to a booking

Any change (date, time, addresses, number of passengers, vehicle) must be requested in writing. Prestigia accepts it subject to availability; the price may be adjusted and a new quote may be issued.

11. Cancellation by the client

Any cancellation must be made in writing, by email or WhatsApp. Cancellation is free of charge up to 24 hours before the pick-up time, in which case the deposit is refunded. If cancelled less than 24 hours before the pick-up time, 100% of the service is due.

12. Client no-show

If the client does not show up at the pick-up point by the end of the included waiting time without notifying Prestigia, 100% of the service is due and no refund is given.

13. Cancellation by Prestigia

If Prestigia has to cancel a confirmed service, it informs the client without delay and offers an equivalent solution or a full refund of the amounts paid for that service.

14. Delays and force majeure

Prestigia tracks flights and trains to adjust the pick-up time. It cannot be held liable for any delay or inability to perform caused by circumstances beyond its control: exceptional traffic conditions, bad weather, strikes, accidents, decisions by the authorities or any other case of force majeure.

15. Vehicles

Services are provided with high-end vehicles that are regularly maintained and covered by the insurance required by law. The model stated in the quote is indicative: Prestigia may replace it with a vehicle of an equivalent or higher category.

16. Chauffeurs and route

The chauffeur chooses the most suitable route, unless the client has made a specific request that was accepted in advance. The chauffeur complies with traffic rules and may refuse any request contrary to the law or to safety.

17. Passengers, children and luggage

The client provides accurate information: addresses, times, flight or train number, number of passengers and items of luggage, any need for child seats. The client ensures that passengers respect the chauffeur, the vehicle and the law, in particular by wearing seat belts.

The number of passengers and items of luggage may not exceed the vehicle capacity agreed at booking. Children travel in a seat suited to their height and weight, to be requested at booking. Animals are only accepted with Prestigia's prior agreement.

18. Conduct on board

Smoking, including e-cigarettes, is prohibited in the vehicles. The client is responsible for the conduct of the passengers they have designated.

Damage to the vehicle caused by a passenger, as well as exceptional cleaning costs, may be charged. Prestigia may refuse or interrupt a service if the safety of the chauffeur, passengers or vehicle is at risk.

19. Lost property

Items left in a vehicle are kept for a reasonable period and returned on request. Any return shipping costs are borne by the client. Prestigia cannot be held liable for items left in a vehicle.

20. Self-drive car rental

Self-drive rental is subject to a separate rental agreement, which sets out in particular the age and driving licence requirements, the security deposit, insurance and the condition report of the vehicle.

21. Private tours

"Private transport" option: the client arranges their own admission tickets. "Turnkey visit" option: Prestigia books and pays for the admission tickets and time slots stated in the quote; meals and personal expenses remain the client's responsibility. A professional guide can be added as an option, on quotation. The chauffeur is not a guide.

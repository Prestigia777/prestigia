Your mobility partner

Everyone in your company, looked after

A client meeting, a board meeting, a seminar or a foreign delegation: Prestigia organises the travel of everyone connected with your company, with the same punctuality and discretion.

  • Your guests and clients: welcome at the airport or station, transfers to your offices, the hotel or dinner.
  • Your executives and CEOs: a discreet chauffeur, journeys planned around their schedule, last-minute changes absorbed.
  • Your managers and senior staff: meetings, roadshows and travel in Belgium and across Europe.
  • Your employees: seminars, corporate events and group travel.

A dedicated contact centralises your requests, and your bookings are handled as a priority.

How it works

Your corporate account in four steps

1. Opening your account

Together we agree on your needs, your contacts and the invoicing terms.

2. Booking

You, your assistant or your teams book by email, phone or WhatsApp. We confirm every journey.

3. Real-time tracking

Your contact is notified by WhatsApp and email at every stage: on the way, on site, picked up, arrived. If the passenger does not show up, you are informed immediately.

4. Monthly invoice

A single detailed invoice each month, journey by journey, ready for your accounts.

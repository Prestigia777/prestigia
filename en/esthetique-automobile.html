Our services

From detailing to servicing

Cleaning & detailing

Interior and exterior: upholstery, leather, bodywork, wheels — meticulous care by our partners, specialists in high-end finishes.

PPF paint protection

A transparent protective film applied to the bodywork by our partners, guarding against stone chips, scratches and everyday knocks.

Car wrapping

A colour change, matte or satin finish, personalised details applied by our partners: a new look, without touching the original paintwork.

Mechanical servicing

Services, oil changes, brakes, tyres: the routine maintenance of your vehicle, entrusted to trusted partner garages.

MOT inspection

We take your vehicle to its MOT inspection on your behalf and return it to you once it has been completed.

Collection & delivery

As an option: we collect your vehicle from your home or office and deliver it back once the work is complete.

Concierge option

We collect your vehicle, and we bring it back

You don't need to go anywhere: a Prestigia chauffeur collects your vehicle from your home or office, drives it to the appropriate partner and returns it once the work is complete.

Thanks to our partners — garages, body shops, inspection centres and detailing specialists — your vehicle benefits from priority access.

  • Collection from your home or office
  • Priority access with our partners
  • Return to the address of your choice
  • A single point of contact, from start to finish
Our role

Partners chosen to the same standard as our fleet

Prestigia selects specialist partners for you — garages, body shops, inspection centres and detailing specialists — with the same high standards we apply to our own vehicles.

We take care of everything else: booking the appointment, coordinating with the partner and, if needed, moving your vehicle there and back.

  • Selected partners throughout Belgium, and in neighbouring countries on request
  • Suited to luxury and collector vehicles
  • One-off services or regular maintenance
  • A single point of contact: Prestigia

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Prestigia SRL, Romeinse Steenweg 265, 1800 Vilvoorde, Belgium (CBE BE 1015.973.545), is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and in the course of its services. For any questions: info@prestigia.vip.

This policy explains what data we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it and how you can exercise your rights, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Belgian Act of 30 July 2018.

1. Data collected

  • your contact details: name, phone number, email address and, where applicable, your company or agency;
  • the details of your request: pick-up and destination addresses, dates and times, flight or train number, number of passengers;
  • for vehicle services: the vehicle concerned and its address;
  • the messages we exchange with you by email, phone or WhatsApp;
  • billing data: name or company name, billing address, VAT number for business clients;
  • limited technical data, such as your IP address, automatically logged by the website host to ensure its operation and security;
  • any other information you choose to share with us.

We do not ask for any sensitive data (health, opinions, origin, etc.). If you tell us about a specific need, such as assistance for a passenger, we use it solely to organise the service.

2. Where the data comes from

Data is generally provided by you, via the website form, by email, by phone or via WhatsApp. When a company or travel agency books for its guests, employees or travellers, it provides us with their data: it undertakes to inform them and to share only the information needed for the journey.

3. Use and legal bases

  • responding to your quote requests (pre-contractual measures);
  • organising and performing the service, and keeping the designated contact person informed (performance of the contract);
  • issuing invoices and meeting our accounting obligations (legal obligation);
  • ensuring the proper functioning and security of the website (legitimate interest).

We do not send you advertising messages without your consent.

The information requested in the form is needed to respond to you: without it, we cannot prepare a quote or organise the service.

4. Recipients

Your data is shared only with those who need it to carry out the service:

  • our chauffeurs;
  • the partners who carry out the service requested (vehicle services, accommodation, ticketing, guides), limited to the information they need;
  • the contact person designated by the company or agency, who receives the stages of the journey: chauffeur on the way, on site, passenger picked up, arrival, passenger no-show;
  • our technical service providers (website hosting, email, WhatsApp) and our accountant;
  • the authorities, where required by law.

Your data is never sold.

5. Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers, such as the website host (Netlify) or WhatsApp, may process data outside the European Union, in particular in the United States. These transfers are covered by the safeguards provided for by the GDPR: an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention period

  • quote requests not followed up: deleted as soon as they are no longer needed;
  • data relating to a service: for the duration of the business relationship, then seven years for accounting records, as required by law.

In the event of a dispute, some data may be kept longer, for as long as necessary to defend our rights.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data against loss, unauthorised access or disclosure: access limited to those who need it, encrypted connection to the website (HTTPS), and partners chosen for their reliability.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • right of access: to know what data we hold about you and obtain a copy;
  • right to rectification: to have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • right to erasure: to request deletion of your data, unless the law requires us to keep it;
  • right to restriction: to request that the use of your data be temporarily suspended;
  • right to object: to object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • right to data portability: to receive the data you have provided in a common format, in order to transfer it to another provider.

To exercise these rights, write to us at info@prestigia.vip. We may ask you to prove your identity. We reply within one month, which may be extended by two months for complex requests; you will be informed if so.

You may also lodge a complaint with theBelgian Data Protection Authority, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels.

9. Automated decision-making

We do not make any decisions based solely on automated processing, and we do not carry out profiling.

10. Minors

Our services are intended for adults. When a minor travels, the booking is made by a responsible adult, who provides only the information needed for the journey.

11. Cookies and third-party services

This website uses neither advertising cookies nor audience measurement tools. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and some images from third-party servers: as with any web page, your IP address is transmitted to them.

When you click a WhatsApp link or a link to a social network, you leave this website: the privacy policy of that service then applies.

12. Changes

We may update this policy, in particular to reflect changes to our services or to the law. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.

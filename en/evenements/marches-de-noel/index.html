Private chauffeur for your Christmas markets in Belgium

Prestigia is the way to visit Belgium's Christmas markets in comfort, with no worries about parking or night driving in winter. Whether you want a private chauffeur for a Christmas market in Belgium, a simple Christmas market transfer to Brussels or Bruges, or to tour the markets of several cities in a single evening in a chauffeur-driven car, our chauffeurs drop you as close as possible to each market and bring you home safely.

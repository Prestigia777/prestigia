Private chauffeur for your festivals and concerts in Belgium

Prestigia takes you to Belgium's biggest music festivals, without parking worries or crowded shuttles. Whether you need a private chauffeur for Tomorrowland, a VIP transfer to the Rock Werchter Festivalpark or a festival chauffeur in Belgium for the Gentse Feesten, Pukkelpop or Les Ardentes, our chauffeurs drop you as close to the entrance as possible and collect you at the agreed time, even late at night.

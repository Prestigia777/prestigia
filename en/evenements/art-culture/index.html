Private chauffeur for your art & culture events in Belgium

Prestigia accompanies collectors, gallery owners and art lovers to Belgium's major art events. Whether you need a private chauffeur for Brafa, a VIP transfer for Art Brussels between the stands at Tour & Taxis or a chauffeur for an art fair in Brussels, our discreet chauffeurs and high-end fleet accompany you with elegance, from one exhibition to the next.

Private chauffeur for your trade fairs in Brussels

Prestigia is the private chauffeur of choice for exhibitors, trade visitors and delegations attending the major fairs in Brussels. Whether you need a private chauffeur for a trade fair in Brussels, a VIP transfer to Brussels Expo between several meetings or a chauffeur for the Brussels Motor Show, Batibouw or Brafa, our professional chauffeurs and high-end fleet spare you the search for parking and time lost between halls.

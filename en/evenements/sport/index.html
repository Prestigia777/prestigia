Private chauffeur for your sporting events in Belgium

Prestigia is your private chauffeur for the Spa Grand Prix and all of Belgium's major sporting events. Whether you are planning a VIP F1 transfer to the Spa-Francorchamps circuit, a trip to a Flemish cycling classic or a private chauffeur for a sporting event in Brussels, our professional chauffeurs and high-end fleet accompany you with punctuality and discretion, in Belgium and neighbouring countries.

For travel agencies

Transfers, activities, tours and visits, accommodation: Prestigia looks after your travellers in Belgium and neighbouring countries, in small and large groups alike. Everything is invoiced to the agency, tracked in real time and fully transparent.

Designed for travel agencies

A reliable partner, your travellers in safe hands

Everything invoiced

Your travellers pay nothing on the spot. Each booking receives a single invoice addressed to the agency, itemised service by service: date, route, travellers.

Real-time tracking

Complete transparency

A clear quote approved for every booking, with no hidden costs or surprises on the invoice. What you approved is what you pay.

What we take care of

Your travellers in good hands, from arrival to departure

Welcome at the airport or station, circuits between Brussels, Bruges, Ghent and Antwerp, activity days, excursions to neighbouring countries: Prestigia organises your clients' travel throughout Belgium and beyond.

We can also book accommodation for your groups. From a handful of travellers to an organised group, we adapt the vehicles and coordinate several chauffeurs. A dedicated contact follows your bookings and handles the unexpected, and your requests are handled as a priority.

  • Airport & station transfers, on arrival and departure
  • Tours and visits: private transport or turnkey visit (tickets and bookings handled by us, as an option)
  • Activities and events during the stay
  • Accommodation booking for your groups
How it works

Your bookings in four steps

1. Your booking

You send us the programme, the number of travellers and the timings. We send you a detailed quote.

2. Organisation

Once the quote is approved, we book vehicles, chauffeurs and, if needed, visits and accommodation. Every pick-up is confirmed.

3. Real-time tracking

Your contact is notified by WhatsApp and email at every stage: on the way, on site, picked up, arrived. If a traveller does not show up, you are informed immediately.

4. One invoice per booking

A single invoice for the booking, itemised service by service and issued on the terms agreed together, easy to match with your own client invoice.
